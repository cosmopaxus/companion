# Equilibrium · Release 3.2 (Oct 18, 2026)

A guided Compass reading. Source: branch `guided-compass`, commit `a65f89d`. Build stamp `mvdztign`.

**Tap Refresh on every device after this publish.**

- **Your teacher asks once a day** on Home: "Shall we take your reading?" Begin, or Later (until tomorrow, on that device).
- **One element at a time:** Fire, Air, Water and Earth arrive in turn, each brought by its familiar (Salamander, Sylph, Undine, Gnome) with one question.
- **Motion and sound:** each element has its own entrance, exit and sound. Sound follows your sound setting; under reduced motion the cards only fade.
- **Back** revisits an element; leaving before Save keeps nothing. The summary blooms the orb in with your reading, then you're back where you started.
- **Rating sliders** are one clean line now, without dots.
- **No data changes:** still `companion.v2`, schema 3. Readings are saved and worked out exactly as before.
