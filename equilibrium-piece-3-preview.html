Where the build still differs

1 · The top bar

Every build screen has the Equilibrium bar (wordmark, Personal, gems) above the art. In the mockups the painting runs to the top of the phone. This is the biggest visible difference.

2 · Check-in is taller

The build keeps a question under each slider and an intro line, so the third slider sits below the fold. The mockup fits all three plus the note on one screen.

3 · Map sits inside the Journey page

In the mockup the map fills the screen with its own header and legend. In the build it sits below the teacher card and the Foundation band.

4 · Guide and seal art

The Undine avatar and the four seal medallions arrive in piece 4.

5 · Sample words differ

The mockups use example content (“Keep a rhythm”, “128 gems”). The build shows your real lessons and numbers.
